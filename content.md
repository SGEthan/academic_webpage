I have a general interest in Computer Vision and Generative Models, for both the image and language domains. Within Computer Vision, I focus on 3D tasks, such as pose estimation, scene understanding, and reconstruction. Regarding language models, I am interested in training and fine-tuning techniques from both theoretical and engineering perspectives.

Previously, during my undergraduate studies and the first two years of my Ph.D., I focused on Theoretical Computer Science, conducting research in data structures, numerical linear algebra, and algorithms. I earned my B.Eng. from the School of the Gifted Young at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC).
